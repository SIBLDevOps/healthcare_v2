1. WHAT IS THIS PROJECT? (IN SIMPLE WORDS)
===============================================================================================

The Lab Tests module lets a person book medical lab tests (blood tests, health packages,
scans) for themselves and their family, from partner labs near them.

In short, a user can:
  - Choose their location (pincode) and how they want the test done:
      * Home Sample Collection - a technician comes home to take the sample, or
      * Visit Lab Centre       - the user goes to a partner lab.
  - Search tests, open health packages, or upload a doctor's prescription.
  - Add tests to a "Lab Cart" and choose WHICH family member each test is for.
  - Compare partner labs (price, rating, report time) and pick one.
  - Pick a home address (or a lab centre), a date and a time slot.
  - Apply a coupon, pay, and get a booking confirmation.
  - Track the booking (technician, sample, report), reschedule or cancel it.
  - View and download reports for each family member.

It is a "multi-lab marketplace": many labs, one website, user chooses the lab.

Important: right now everything works with DUMMY (sample) data saved in the browser.
No real server, payment or SMS is used yet. Section 18 explains how to connect a real backend.

6. HOW THE CODE WORKS (ARCHITECTURE)
===============================================================================================

   +-------------------+       +------------------+       +---------------------+
   |  data/*.js        | ----> |  core/api.js     | ----> |  pages/*.js         |
   |  (dummy database) |       |  (fake server,   |       |  (draws each page)  |
   +-------------------+       |   LT.api.*)      |       +----------+----------+
                               +--------+---------+                  |
                                        |                            v
                               +--------v---------+       +---------------------+
                               |  core/store.js   | <---> |  core/layout.js     |
                               |  (browser memory:|       |  core/ui.js         |
                               |   cart, user...) |       |  core/members-ui.js |
                               +------------------+       |  core/guards.js     |
                                                          +---------------------+

  - Every page asks LT.api for data (like a real server). LT.api answers after a short
    delay, like a real network call, and can show errors (Demo tools -> API error).
  - LT.store keeps the current booking in the browser (location, mode, cart, members,
    chosen lab, address, slot, coupon). It survives page refresh.
  - LT.db (inside store.js) keeps "server-side" demo records: members, addresses,
    bookings, reports, requests, labs status.
  - guards.js makes sure the order of steps is followed. Example: if someone opens
    slot.html without choosing a lab, they are sent to compare-labs.html with a message.
  - All global objects start with "LT." (Lab Tests) so they never clash with the main site.

STEP-BY-STEP (what the user does and sees)

Step 1 - Open the Lab Tests page
  The first time, the "Select your location" popup opens.

Step 2 - Choose location and collection type (ONE popup)
  - Type an area, city or 6-digit pincode in the search box, or tap
    "Use my current location". (There is no list of popular cities - search only.)
  - Suggestions appear as you type (they float above the popup, never cut off).
  - A "service card" shows the result:
      * Green  "All services available"   - two cards: Home Sample Collection and Visit Lab
                                            Centre. The user taps one. Continue is enabled
                                            after a choice.
      * Amber  "Some services available"  - the available one is "Selected for you".
      * Red    "Service not available at your location" - only Pune and Mumbai are served.
               Buttons: "Pune" / "Mumbai" (searches that city) and "Change location".
               Optional "Get notified when we launch" (mobile or email).
  - Continue saves location + type and opens the landing page.
  - Change later from the header (location chip / Home-Lab switch).

Step 3 - Find tests
  Search bar, categories, popular tests, health packages, or upload a prescription.

Step 4 - Add a test
  - Not logged in? The "Login to continue" popup opens first (Login / Register tabs).
  - There is NO member popup. The test goes straight into the lab cart for "Self" and a toast
    says "CBC added for Riya. Book it for family members in your cart." (+ "View cart").
  - If the test is not suitable for Self (e.g. PSA is for men only) it is added without a
    member; the Lab Cart then asks who it is for.
  - Tapping "Added" on a card removes the test again (toast with Undo).
  - Family members are chosen on the Lab Cart page (see "Lab Cart" below).
  - On the FIRST test the LAB SIDE-BAR opens (Step 5).

Step 5 - Lab side-bar "Choose a lab" (opens from the right; full screen on phones)
  - Opens only for the FIRST test of a booking (when no lab is chosen yet). Later tests use
    the chosen lab without asking; the lab is changed on the Compare labs page. The lab is
    cleared when the cart becomes empty (or the mode changes / logout), so the next first
    test asks again.
  - The test at the top with who it is for ("For Riya, Karan" + Change).
  - Every lab near the user as a SMALL card: logo, name, rating, price for THIS test
    (MRP and % off) and a "Select" button. With 2+ members: "Rs.413 x 2 = Rs.826".
    With more tests in the cart: "Cart here Rs.X" (+ "1 n/a" when a test is missing).
    "Earlier lab" marks the lab chosen before.
  - Sort: Recommended / Lowest price / Fastest report / Top rated (/ Nearest).
  - Labs that do not do the test are NOT listed.
  - Centre-only test in home mode: message + "Switch to Lab Centre" button.
  - "Select" saves the lab and CLOSES the side-bar; the user stays on the same page.
    A toast confirms it (with a "Compare labs" shortcut).
  - Lab tag on test / package cards: once a lab is chosen, EVERY test and package card
    (landing, search, packages, test detail price card) shows the lab name as a small green
    tag on the card's top-right corner - your tests are done by that lab. A test the lab
    does not do shows an amber "Not at <lab>" tag (and a warning toast if it is added:
    "change the lab on the Compare labs page"). No lab chosen = no tags.

Step 6 - Compare labs page (compare-labs.html = the "Lab" step in the step bar)
  - Top: "Your selected lab" - the lab chosen in the side-bar when the first test was
    added (a short note says so). Only when no lab was chosen: "Best match for your tests".
      * "Tests & prices" drop-down (opens / closes like an FAQ; open on desktop, closed on
        tablet and phone): every test with WHO it is for (member name chips, e.g.
        "Riya" "Karan") and its price ("Rs.449 x 2" for two people).
      * Footer row: total payable (MRP struck, "You save"), who it is for, and
        "Select & Continue" (full width on phones).
  - Below: "Compare with other labs" - one card per lab: "All 3 tests" or "2 of 3 tests"
    badge, its own test drop-down (closed by default; the summary line already says e.g.
    "Vitamin B12 not available"), total, and
    "Rs.222 less / Rs.80 more than your lab" when both cover the same tests.
  - Page head chips: location, mode, members and tests, "Add more tests", "Edit tests".
  - Sort: Price (default) / Best match / Most tests / Fastest / Top rated (/ Nearest).
    Filters: All tests available, NABL/CAP, Report within 24 h, Free collection.
  - "Select & Continue" on a lab that misses tests opens a popup:
      "This lab offers only 2 of your 3 tests" - Available here (with prices) /
      Not available here. Cancel = stay on the Compare page. Continue = the missing tests
      are removed from this booking and the user goes to the Location step.
  - Centre visit + lab with 2+ centres: "Choose a centre" popup first.
  - Changing the lab clears the slot.

Lab Cart (cart.html) - still available any time
  - Top: "Your selected lab" card - lab logo, name, rating, "All 3 tests" / "2 of 3 tests",
    total at this lab, "You save", and a "Change lab" button (opens Compare labs). If the
    lab misses a test, an amber note names it. No lab yet: "No lab selected yet" +
    "Choose a lab".
  - Step 1 "Who is this booking for?": an "Add member" card first, then Self and every
    family member (avatar, name, relationship, age, "N tests" / "Tap to add"). Tap a member to
    book ALL suitable tests for them (ticked card); tap again to remove them from the booking
    (toast with Undo). Incomplete profiles ask for age & gender first. Max 6 members per
    booking. On phones the member cards are one swipeable row. Logged out: "Log in" prompt.
  - Step 2 "Tests in your cart": one card per test / package - name, sample, parameters,
    fasting, report time, source chips, price ("From Rs.245 x 2"), remove (bin), and member
    chips under "FOR" to choose exactly who gets it. Members the test is not suitable for
    are greyed out with the reason (e.g. "PSA is only for men."). A test with nobody ticked
    turns amber: "Choose who this test is for."
  - Tests added before logging in are given to Self automatically the first time.
  - Warnings: centre-only tests in home mode, duplicates per person (Fix now), similar
    variants, fasting needed.
  - "Continue" (to Compare labs) is disabled until every test has a member.

Step 7 - Location step
  - Home Collection: saved addresses are shown ONLY if they match the chosen pincode.
    Addresses from other pincodes are hidden with a note. "+ Add" opens a form; the
    pincode is fixed to the chosen location (with a "Change location" link).
  - Lab Centre: confirm which centre to visit (with map and directions).

Step 8 - Slot (Select Collection Date & Time)
  - Header: "For <members> - <lab name>".
  - 7 date pills (example FRI 20, SAT 21). The selected day shows "13 Slots".
    Days with no slots show "Full".
  - "MORNING SLOTS" (sun icon) with a "Fasting Recommended" badge when a test needs
    fasting: 05:00-06:00 AM, 06:00-07:00 AM ... 11:00 AM-12:00 PM (7 windows).
  - "AFTERNOON & EVENING" (moon icon): 12:00-01:00 PM, 01:00-02:00 PM, 02:00-03:00 PM,
    04:00-05:00 PM, 05:00-06:00 PM, 06:00-07:00 PM (6 windows; no 3-4 PM slot).
  - 13 one-hour windows a day, 2 per row on phones and 3 on desktop. Only one slot can be
    selected across both groups. A group whose slots have all passed today says so.
  - Same-day slots need 2 hours notice (earlier ones are greyed out).
  - "Confirm Slot (Tue, 24 Nov - 06:00 AM)" holds the slot for 10 minutes.

Step 9 - Summary and Pay
  - Part 1: members and tests, prescription, location, lab, slot, bill, consent.
    Coupon: inside Bill details, right under "Taxes (diagnostics exempt)" - an "Enter coupon
    code" box with Apply (Enter key works). Errors show under the box (empty / invalid /
    expired / not applicable). Applied: green "CODE applied - You saved Rs.X" + Remove.
    (The separate "Coupons & offers" card was removed.)
  - Part 2: payment method - UPI, Card, Net banking, Wallet, or Pay at collection/centre.
  - A countdown shows the slot hold time; if it expires, the user picks a slot again.

Step 10 - Confirmation
  - Booking ID (copy), summary, preparation tips.
  - Add to calendar (.ics file), invoice, share, view appointment, book another.

Step 11 - After booking
  - My Appointments -> open a booking -> see the live tracking timeline.
  - Reports become available per family member when ready.

9. BUSINESS RULES
===============================================================================================

Service area
  - Served cities: ONLY Pune and Mumbai.
      411057 (Wakad, Hinjewadi), 411045 (Baner), 411038 (Kothrud),
      400001 (Fort), 400069 (Andheri East)  -> Home Collection + Lab Centre
      411014 (Viman Nagar)                 -> Home Collection only
      411005 (Shivajinagar)                -> Lab Centre only
  - Not served (appear in search with "Service not available"): Delhi, Bangalore,
    Hyderabad, Chennai, Kolkata, Ahmedabad, Dhule, Nashik, Nagpur, Jaipur, and any
    pincode not in the list.
  - If a saved location stops being served, the home page asks for a new location and
    booking pages redirect back (guards.js).

Collection type
  - Imaging tests (Ultrasound, X-Ray, ECG) are Lab Centre only; they are blocked in
    Home mode.
  - Switching type resets the chosen lab, address/centre and slot (cart stays).

Prices
  - MRP (printed price) is the same at every lab. Each lab sells at its own price
    (lab price factor), so the user can compare.
  - Lab tests are GST-exempt, so tax shows Rs.0.
  - Collection charge is charged once per booking (some labs give it free above an
    amount).

Coupons (demo)
  CARE15     15% off up to Rs.300, on orders above Rs.499
  FIRSTMED   Flat Rs.120 off, first lab booking only
  HEALTH100  Flat Rs.100 off, on orders above Rs.999
  HOMEONLY   10% off home collection (up to Rs.200), home mode only
  EXPIRED10  Expired coupon - shows the "expired" error (for testing)

Members
  - Every test in the cart must belong to at least one family member.
  - Maximum 6 members per booking.
  - Gender / age rules (example: PSA is only for men aged 40 or more; some other tests
    have a minimum age, e.g. 12+). Members who do not match are disabled with the reason.
  - The "Self" member's relationship cannot be changed.

Slots
  - 13 one-hour windows a day for every lab: 7 morning (05:00 AM - 12:00 PM) and 6 afternoon &
    evening (12:00 - 07:00 PM, no 3-4 PM slot).
  - Same-day slots need at least 2 hours notice.
  - A chosen slot is held for 10 minutes until payment.

Booking and payment
  - A booking starts as "pending payment" and becomes "confirmed" after successful
    payment. "Pay at collection / centre" confirms straight away.

Changes and refunds
  - Reschedule: same lab only, maximum 2 times, not within 2 hours of the slot.
  - Changes close 2 hours before the slot or once the technician is on the way.
  - Refund on cancel: more than 12 hours before the slot -> 100%;
                      2 to 12 hours before   -> 50%;
                      within 2 hours         -> no refund.
  - Refunds go back to the original payment method in 5-7 working days.

20. GLOSSARY (SIMPLE MEANING OF WORDS USED)
===============================================================================================

  Home Sample Collection - a technician (phlebotomist) visits home to take the sample.
  Lab Centre Visit       - the user goes to the partner lab centre.
  Pincode                - 6-digit Indian postal code used to check service area.
  Serviceable            - we can provide the service at that pincode.
  MRP                    - maximum retail (printed) price of a test.
  Package                - a group of tests sold together at a lower price.
  Fasting                - not eating for some hours before the test.
  TAT                    - turnaround time: how long until the report is ready.
  NABL / CAP             - quality accreditations of labs.
  Slot hold              - the chosen time is reserved for 10 minutes until payment.
  Guard                  - a check that sends the user back if a required step is missing.
  Bottom sheet           - a popup that slides up from the bottom on mobile.
  Demo tools             - testing shortcuts; not part of the final product.
